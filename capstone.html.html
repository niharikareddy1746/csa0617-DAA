Mathematical Formulation & Cold-Chain Constraints

NP-Complete • Pseudo-Polynomial O(nW)
// 1. Objective Function: Maximize total public health priority score
Maximize Z = Σi=1..n ( vi · xi )

// 2. Cold-Chain Container Volume Constraint
Subject to Σi=1..n ( wi · xi ) ≤ W   (W = Cold-box capacity in Liters)
           Ti ∈ Tallowed       (Thermal compatibility constraint)
           xi ∈ {0, 1}         (Binary decision: whole sealed batch or reject)

// 3. Bellman Recurrence Relation
DP[i][w] = DP[i-1][w]                                   if wi > w
DP[i][w] = max( DP[i-1][w],  vi + DP[i-1][w - wi] )    if wi ≤ w

Why 0/1 Knapsack (Not Fractional)?

Vaccine vials are packaged in tamper-evident, insulated thermal carriers. Breaking a sealed secondary carton compromises cold-chain stability and sterility, requiring an indivisible binary decision $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$.

Priority Score ($v_i$) Computation

Each candidate batch value $v_i$ combines target population vulnerability, regional outbreak incidence rate, and remaining shelf-life urgency (FEFO — First-Expiry, First-Out).

Time & Space Complexity

Bottom-up tabulation computes optimal subproblems in $\mathcal{O}(n \cdot W)$ time and $\mathcal{O}(n \cdot W)$ space, enabling full decision backtracking in $\mathcal{O}(n)$ steps.
